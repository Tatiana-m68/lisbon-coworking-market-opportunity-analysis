# Project Brief

## Decision
Help the Expansion Director narrow the search for one new coworking location from Lisbon municipality's 24 civil parishes to three areas where real property offers and local business conditions can be compared in parallel.

## Business and data questions
1. **Competition:** How many active coworking locations are observed in each parish and per 10,000 working-age residents?
2. **Demand:** How do working-age population, business POIs, universities, hotels and cafés vary by parish?
3. **Accessibility:** How many metro/rail stations and selected transit stops are available per km² or within a defined buffer?
4. **Value:** How does median asking rent per m² compare with demand proxies and observed competition?
5. **Recommendation:** Which three parishes form the strongest and most stable shortlist across plausible weighting scenarios?

## Initial hypothesis
A small group of parishes combining strong demand proxies, high transit accessibility, manageable competition and sustainable asking-rent levels will provide the strongest search area for closer local analysis.

## Successful project output

The project is successful when it identifies a clear three-area shortlist,
explains the trade-offs in each area and gives practical next steps for
comparing real opportunities. The output is one search scope, not a strict
internal ranking.

## Main caveat
The composite score is a transparent screening tool, not a forecast of occupancy or profitability. Public data cannot directly reveal willingness to pay, unit economics, lease incentives or future supply.
